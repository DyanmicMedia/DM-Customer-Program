Make a personal link

Each person gets their own link. When they open it, the team gets an email and the visit shows up under Activity.

How to send it

  1. Copy the personal link.
  2. In Outlook, select the words you want as the link, press Ctrl+K, paste, and click OK.
  3. Send. Don’t open the link yourself from that browser unless it’s excluded (see This browser).

The share link is for the person to forward. Visits through it are labeled as forwarded by them, so you can tell their own reading apart from the people they sent it to.

This browser
