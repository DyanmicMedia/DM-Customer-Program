03 · We did our homework

Competition pedigree.
Backyard hospitality.

The food discipline is real, but the culture is the product. You sell the belonging of a family reunion, not the swagger of a Texas honky-tonk. The music has to do the same.

Your brand, in your words

  • “Serving & creating happiness”

    Cheerful and warm. Never brooding.

  • “Genuine backyard hospitality”

    Familiar songs. A cookout, not a bar.

  • “The flavor of America”

    Every region on the menu, so every region in the music.

  • Locations called “joints”

    Unpretentious. No lounge polish.

Your guests, by daypart

  • The lunch-break crew

    12–2 PM

    Momentum without stress. This is the money daypart.

  • The catering planner

    2–5 PM

    Should sound like the brand they’re about to put their name on.

  • The weeknight family

    5–8 PM

    Warm, with room to talk. Nothing a parent has to explain in the car.

  • The BBQ enthusiast

    Weekends

    Notices everything. The craft story has to hold up in the speakers too.

So it sounds like

The flavor of America, by ear.

  1. Texas

    Country & red dirt

  2. Memphis

    Soul

  3. Chicago

    Electric blues

  4. Carolinas

    Folk & string band

  5. NOLA

    Funk & R&B

  6. Bakersfield

    Honky-tonk country

Never: explicit lyrics, Nashville-bro country, synth-heavy pop, lounge or chill-hop, or whatever’s trending this week.
