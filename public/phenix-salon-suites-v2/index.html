01

Summary

Phenix franchisees choose their own vendors, and Phenix prefers to give them more than one endorsed option. Today, Custom Channels is the single music option offered. This proposal adds a second: Soundtrack, delivered by Dynamic Media.

Franchisees get a lower price than they could find on their own and a Phenix-approved music library. Phenix corporate earns a revenue share on every active location, takes on no billing or collections, and gets a direct messaging channel to salon professionals in every participating store.

ProposalSoundtrack becomes a Phenix-endorsed music provider, offered alongside Custom Channels.
Franchisee price$26.95 per location per month (Phenix family rate). The standard price is $41.00.
Revenue to Phenix$2.00 per active location per month, paid quarterly, plus 10% of equipment purchased through Dynamic Media.
Cost to Phenix corporateNone. Dynamic Media bills each franchisee directly. Phenix never handles payments.
TermTwo-year agreement with Phenix corporate. Franchisees are month to month.
Phenix’s roleEndorse the program, make franchisees aware of it, and approve the music library and messages.

02

What Soundtrack is

Soundtrack began as Spotify’s business offering and was rebuilt specifically for use in business. It has the depth of a personal streaming service with the licensing and controls a business needs. It’s the fastest-growing business music platform in the world.

Licensing matters because personal streaming accounts aren’t licensed for public play in a business. Soundtrack includes the licensing in the monthly price.

A full music catalog

More than 125 million songs, organized for business use.

Playlists that keep themselves fresh

About 1,500 professionally curated business playlists, updated every few days, so locations don’t hear repeats.

A corporate-approved library

Phenix decides which playlists are available. Franchisees choose from that list and nothing else.

One dashboard

Each location appears in a web portal with what’s playing and whether its player is online. Multi-unit owners see all their salons in one login.

Schedules

Music can change by day and time, and seasonal music like the holidays can be blended in automatically.

Set it and forget it

Once a salon is set up, nobody has to manage the music.

How the partnership works

The structure matches Phenix’s other endorsed programs: one agreement with corporate, and a simple month-to-month service for each franchisee. The program depends on franchisees hearing about it from Phenix, so awareness is Phenix’s main role.

Phenix corporate

  • Sets up Dynamic Media as an endorsed vendor
  • Tells franchisees about the program through Franchise Connection, e-blasts, and franchise calls, at launch and after
  • Approves the music library and messages

Dynamic Media

  • Bills each franchisee directly
  • Onboards and sets up every salon
  • Builds the Phenix library and Gina’s List
  • Scripts and produces in-store messages when Phenix wants
  • Runs the program website and support

Franchisees

  • Sign up on the program website
  • Pick from Phenix-approved playlists
  • Play on the equipment they already have
  • Cancel any month, with no penalty

07

In-store messaging

Messaging plays short recorded announcements between songs, scheduled by Phenix corporate by date, time, and location.

Most businesses use it to reach a customer who’s in the store for an hour. At Phenix, the audience is the salon professionals, who are in the building all day and pass through the lobby, break room, and laundry room. It gives Phenix a steady channel for the programs it already runs for them.

What a Phenix campaign could include

  • Programs for pros. Education, health care, and insurance.
  • Brand partner offers. Product deals with partners like L’Oréal and Redken.
  • Philanthropy. Grants for professionals and community events.
  • Brand story. Who Phenix is and what it stands for.
  • News. New programs as they launch.

How it runs

  • Frequency. Start at one message every 20 minutes, so it never sounds like radio.
  • Volume. Messages are unlimited.
  • Production. Phenix marketing can produce messages in house, or have Dynamic Media script and record them.
  • Approval. Phenix approves every message before it plays.
  • Testing. Corporate stores first, then adjust.

Message production

Phenix decides who makes its messages. Its marketing team can write and record them internally, with Dynamic Media loading them into the schedule, or Dynamic Media can handle the scripting and professional voice production. Phenix can mix the two.

Phenix owns every message. Once a message is produced, including the six kickoff messages and any message Phenix pays $99 for, it belongs to Phenix and becomes part of Phenix’s own message library, to schedule and reuse whenever it wants.

09

A first look at the Phenix sound

This is a first look at the music we would recommend: how we read the Phenix brand, how that translates into sound, and five ready-made Soundtrack playlists we think are a good place to start. The full brand brief and Phenix’s approval come next.

How we see the brand

Phenix isn’t selling a haircut. It’s selling the moment a stylist stops renting a chair and becomes an owner.

Each building is 40 to 60 private suites, every one designed by its own professional: colorists, barbers, braiders, estheticians, lash artists, and nail techs, each playing their own music behind insulated walls. The lobby and hallway are the one space Phenix controls. At its best, that space feels like a well-run boutique office for creatives: clean, a little glamorous, and proud of the people behind each door. The music has to belong to the whole building, not to any one suite.

How that translates into music

It should sound

  • Polished. The hallway is the leasing showroom.
  • Warm. Pros call their suite a second home.
  • Contemporary. Tenants are building modern personal brands.
  • Confident. An ownership story, not a service counter.
  • Inclusive. Every specialty and every client walks the same corridor.
  • Unobtrusive. It sits under fifty suites’ own music without clashing.

It should not sound

  • Like a spa. Most of the building is lively hair work.
  • Like a club. Heavy bass bleeds into color consultations and lash work.
  • Explicit. Kids’ cuts and bridal parties share the same hour.
  • Like one genre. A single scene would feel like it belongs to one tenant.
  • Dated. The prospective tenant is deciding whether the building is current.

Five playlists to start, by time of day

Each link opens the playlist on Soundtrack with its full song list; no account is needed to browse. Phenix locations would play with explicit songs filtered out. Gina’s List and the approved Phenix library build from this starting point.

Audio systems for new stores

Phenix has 209 stores in the pipeline. Audio planned during construction costs less than audio added after opening, so we propose joining the new-store process early, alongside real estate and project management.

Because Dynamic Media manufactures its own audio systems, they typically come in about 40% below retail. Phenix earns 10% of every system purchased.

Why factory direct

  • About 40% below retail. We design and manufacture the equipment, so there’s no distributor or reseller markup.
  • Built for commercial use. Amplifiers and speakers made to run all day, every day.
  • One standard for every store. The same components in every new Phenix location, sized to each floor plan.
  • Designed at no charge. We lay out the system from the floor plan before construction.
  • Installed nationwide. Our network of 8,500 technicians covers the entire US.
  • One company to call. The same team supports the music and the hardware.
