Program & Pricing

Is Woof Gang Radio the official music program? +
Yes. Woof Gang Radio is part of the standard opening package for new Woof Gang Bakery & Grooming stores. Every store gets the same licensed, family-friendly music program.
What if my store is already open? +
You can join anytime. Tell us how music plays in your store today. Most open stores keep the speakers they already have. We set up your account, and a 15-minute call with your rep covers payment and your switch-over date. After that, Woof Gang Radio replaces whatever you’re playing now.
What does it cost? +
The music service is $24.95 per month per location. That covers your branded Woof Gang Radio playlist, the library of Woof Gang-approved playlists, commercial music licensing, and program support. If you need speakers, the Woof Gang Speaker Package starts at $1,100, installed. If you already have speakers, we’ll connect to them.
How does billing work? +
Dynamic Media bills each franchisee directly. Your account is created on hold, and nothing is charged until it’s activated. Your rep takes your payment details on your setup call and confirms your activation date then.
What is Soundtrack, and why can’t I use my own Spotify? +
Personal Spotify, Apple Music, and Pandora accounts aren’t licensed for business use. Playing them in a store puts the store at risk with the music licensing organizations. Soundtrack is a commercial music service built for businesses, and the licensing is included. The Spotify playlist on this page is only a listening sample. Listen to the sample →
Can my store choose its own music? +
Yes. Along with your branded Woof Gang Radio playlist, you can play anything from a library of family-friendly, Woof Gang-approved playlists. Every one is on-brand, so you get a real choice without having to screen anything.
I own more than one store. Can I see them all in one place? +
Yes. Multi-unit owners get their own view of all their stores. Because the system is cloud-based, you can change playlists, skip songs, or adjust the volume from anywhere, without being in the store.

Equipment & Setup

Can I use the speakers already in my store? +
Usually, yes. Woof Gang Radio works with Sonos, Bluetooth speakers, a store computer, or an existing wired system.
How long does setup take? +
Confirming your store online takes about two minutes, and the setup call takes about 15. On your activation date, getting the music playing takes about three minutes: sign in to the app or activate the service on your Sonos, then choose a playlist. We send video instructions, and the program team is available if you need help.
Can you supply and install speakers for my store? +
Yes. The Woof Gang Speaker Package is four commercial speakers covering your whole floor, professionally installed, starting at $1,100. Request a quote anytime, and your rep will coordinate installation with your contractor before you open.
What if I don’t know what equipment we have? +
That’s fine. Send a photo of the speaker, amplifier, or current music player, and our team will identify the setup.
Who helps if something isn’t working? +
Dynamic Media supports the music service and your speakers as one program team, and you’ll have a dedicated account executive. Email woofgang@dm-us.com, or see the PlayerOne Quick Start Guide →
