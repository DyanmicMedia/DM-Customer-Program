Program & Pricing

Is Broken Yolk Radio the official corporate music program? +
Yes. Broken Yolk Radio is the corporate music standard for Broken Yolk Cafe locations. It gives every cafe the same professionally managed, fully licensed soundtrack.
What is included in the $27.95 monthly music service? +
The monthly service includes access to Broken Yolk Radio, ongoing playlist management, monthly music updates, commercial music licensing, and program support. Audio equipment and professional installation are handled separately when needed.
How does billing work? +

Each franchisee sets up its program account with Dynamic Media, and Dynamic Media bills the franchisee.

  • Monthly payments can be made by credit card or ACH.
  • Annual payments can be made by credit card, check, or ACH.
What is Soundtrack, and why can’t I use Spotify? +
Soundtrack is the commercially licensed music service that delivers Broken Yolk Radio to your cafe. Personal Spotify accounts are not licensed for business playback; the Spotify playlist on this page is only a listening sample. Listen to the sample →
Can my location choose its own music? +
Broken Yolk Radio is centrally curated and managed so guests hear a consistent brand experience at every cafe. Managers do not need to build or maintain playlists. Music feedback can be sent to the program team.

Equipment & Setup

Can I use the speakers and equipment already in my cafe? +
Usually, yes. Broken Yolk Radio can work with Sonos, Bluetooth speakers, a store computer, or an existing wired system.
What if I do not know what equipment we have? +
That is completely fine. A photo of the speaker, amplifier, or current music player can help our team identify the setup.
What should I do for a new or remodeled location? +
Dynamic Media will create an audio system design at no charge, recommend the speakers, amplifiers, controls, and music player, and coordinate equipment and installation.
Who helps if something is not working? +
Dynamic Media supports the music service, playback equipment, and audio system through one program team. Email brokenyolk@dm-us.com, or use the PlayerOne Quick Start Guide →
