In-store messaging

A direct line to your salon professionals

“Our clients are the franchisees and salon professionals.”

Most businesses use messaging to reach a customer who’s in the store for an hour. Your salon pros are in the building all day, in the lobby, break room, and laundry room. That makes messaging a steady channel for the programs you already run for them.

Messages are unlimited. We’d start with one every 20 minutes so it never sounds like radio, test in corporate stores, and adjust from there.

What a Phenix campaign could include

    Your marketing team can produce messages in house, or we script and record them. The first six branded messages are free at kickoff; additional messages are $99 each. Once a message is made, you own it, and it becomes part of your own message library. You approve every message before it plays.

    Phenix-branded music

    What Phenix sounds like

    It starts with a brand brief. We put into words what Phenix should sound like, what to leave out, and recommend playlists from Soundtrack’s salon collection. You listen, approve, and they go into the Phenix library.

    Then we build signature playlists under the Phenix name, starting with Gina’s List. Most personal playlists aren’t built for a business, so we take Gina’s favorites and grow them into a full playlist that keeps her feel and fits a salon.

    Artists you work with through Priest James Entertainment can be mixed in too, as long as their music is on Spotify and licensed for business use. Typically 85 to 90% of any playlist is.

    Gina’s List is included free

    The first build comes with the signed partnership, live on day one. Later playlists are $1,000 to build and $50 a month to maintain.
