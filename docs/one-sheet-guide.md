# Making a customer one-sheet

A one-sheet is a short web page for a customer: what we're offering, what it costs, and how to start. It uses the same format as the Phenix partnership proposal, and it tells you when the customer opens it and which sections they read.

Sample: https://dm-customer-programs.web.app/one-sheet-template/

## Making one in Cursor

1. Open this project (DM-Customer-Program) in Cursor. Get the latest version first: in the chat, ask "pull the latest from GitHub."
2. In the chat, type `/one-sheet` and paste your call notes: the customer name, who you're sending it to, the prices you quoted, the term, and anything they asked about. It goes out under your name, so confirm your title and phone when asked.
3. Cursor builds the page and gives you a preview link on your computer. Ask for any changes in plain English ("make it month to month," "add PlayerOne," "drop the FAQ").
4. When it looks right, ask Cursor to "package this one-sheet for Ryan." Email Ryan the zip file and the headers file it makes.
5. Ryan publishes it and sends back the live link. Don't publish it yourself: publishing from your copy would replace the whole live site with your copy and take down other customers' pages.

## Sending it

Give each person their own link by adding `?r=` and their name:

`https://dm-customer-programs.web.app/acme-fitness/?r=jane-smith`

- When Jane opens it, the team gets an email alert, and her visit shows up under her name in the activity view at https://dm-customer-programs.web.app/admin/ (ask Ryan for the passcode), with the sections she read and for how long.
- If Jane forwards it, those visits show up as "forwarded from Jane Smith."
- Don't click the customer's link yourself. To check the page, first open https://dm-customer-programs.web.app/one-sheet-template/?notrack=1 once in each browser you use, so your own visits aren't counted.

## Rules

- Use only the prices you quoted on the call. If you're unsure, ask Ryan before sending.
- Plain, short sentences. Nothing the contact would be embarrassed to forward to their boss.
- Every one-sheet is hidden from search engines, but anyone with the link can open it.
