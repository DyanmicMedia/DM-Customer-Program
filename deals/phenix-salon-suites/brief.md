## Current state (from the transcript)
- 427 franchise locations plus about 45–50 corporate stores ("450 plus stores"). About 16,000 salon pros.
- Custom Channels is offered today as the single music source. Ryan recalls about $34.99; Sherrie has to check.
- Music plays in common areas, not inside suites.
- Pilot ran at Austin Highway, Campbell Plaza, and Williams Centre around Christmas, with holiday schedules.
- Phenix has a similar endorsed deal with Scent Air: corporate agreement, franchisees month-to-month, curated "Gina's favorites" scents.

## Pains in their words
- "We don't like to do that. We typically like to multi-source."
- "We don't want to be basically a collection agency for another company."
- "There's a lot of marketing time that goes out behind us to market programs like this."

## Fears (what stalls this deal)
1. Price: "making sure we're at a competitive price point is always really a big deal."
2. Billing: "franchisees can be billed individually."
3. Commitment: "there's always this fear of what if we don't like the program."
4. Staleness: "How do you keep stuff current?"
5. Corporate effort without return: staff time, magazine, e-blast, CEO calls.

## What they want
- Tier 2 model: a corporate-approved library franchisees choose from.
- Corporate messaging (L'Oreal, Redken, national convention).
- Gina's List and other Phenix-branded playlists.
- A percentage back to corporate. "Phenix family pricing" vs direct price.
- Multi-location discount and corporate-store pricing (asked, not yet answered).
- Explore something for the 16,000 salon pros (Ryan to think about it).

## Pricing (proposal, consistent with the call)
- Soundtrack Essential: $26.95 / location / month (MSRP $41.00). The proposal says "Unlimited"; Ryan confirmed it's Essential. Savings $14.05/mo, $168.60/yr.
- Messaging: $6.00 / location / month (quoted on the call).
- Message production (Ryan, Oct 20): six branded kickoff messages free; additional DM-scripted and produced messages $99 each. Phenix marketing may produce its own messages instead. Phenix owns every message once produced; it joins Phenix's message library.
- Fully loaded: $32.95 / month.
- Revenue share: $2.00 / active location / month, paid quarterly; 10% of equipment bought through DM.
- Custom playlist: $1,000 build, $50/mo maintenance. Gina's List build included free at signing.
- Terms: two-year corporate MSA; franchisees month-to-month.
